Five screens at the density a working fashion platform demands.

Built after reading what the benchmarks actually do. Zhiyi runs a structured apparel database — a billion garment images, roughly a thousand attribute tags, retrieval by garment rather than by keyword — so its interface is facets and find-similar, not a prompt box. Centric's Visual Boards put commercial data underneath the image, and its 3D is Connect: CLO, Browzwear and Optitex author, Centric records. Both lessons are in these screens. The fourth joins design intent to a governed Style record. The fifth is what a real pilot sees before the data arrives: useful manual work, explicit absence, and no invented forecast.

Taken from ZhiyiAttribute facets, find similar, and per-attribute commercial read. Retrieval is the interface.
Taken from CentricData under the garment, tabbed product record, and integrate, never author for 3D and CAD.
Refused from bothA single confidence score. Atelier separates observed from inferred and keeps counter-evidence on screen.

Atelier refuses to invent
  • ×A demand forecast without sufficient history and a validated gold set.
  • ×A best supplier without comparable current quotes and attributed performance.
  • ×A margin or target cost from blank commercial inputs.
  • ×A measurement, composition or construction fact inferred from a render.
  • ×A zero where the real state is not connected, not run, or unknown.
